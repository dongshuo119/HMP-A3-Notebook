## Problem

Generating human motion in complex 3D scenes from text is a challenging task with broad applications. However, existing methods often overlook realistic physical contact, resulting in visually plausible but physically unrealistic motion, e.g., penetration.

## Key Idea

To alleviate this, we propose IntentMotion, a novel framework that generates human motion in 3D scenes from natural language instructions by explicitly modeling intent.
