## Problem

Stochastic Human Motion Prediction (HMP) aims to predict multiple possible future human pose sequences from observed ones. Most prior works learn motion distributions through encoding-decoding in the latent space, which does not preserve motion's spatial-temporal structure.

## Key Idea

To address these issues, we propose CoMusion, a single-stage, end-to-end diffusion-based stochastic HMP framework.
